The dispatch board three depots plan their day on

Planning a day of deliveries fell from 40 minutes to 12.

Role
Lead frontend
Team
4 engineers, 1 designer
Time
7 months
Stack
React, Node, DuckDB

Dispatchers were planning on paper

Each depot kept its own spreadsheet, and vans were double-booked most mornings.

The grid had to stay fast at full size

A full week is ten million rows. We tried virtualising the DOM first and rejected it, because grouping forced a full re-layout on every change.

Moving the data into DuckDB in the browser made grouping cheap, and the view only draws what is on screen.

What changed

Planning a day went from 40 minutes to 12, and double-bookings stopped being a daily event.

What I would do differently

I would ask dispatchers to try it in week two instead of week ten. Their first complaint changed the layout.

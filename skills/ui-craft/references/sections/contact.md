# Contact

**Job:** make getting in touch easy, and say what happens next.

| Variant | Use when |
|---|---|
| `direct` | An email link and a few profiles. Often all a portfolio needs |
| `split` | Heading and details left, form right |
| `form-only` | Centred form in `--container-narrow` |
| `visit` | A place: address, hours, map link, phone |

Form rules in `../components/form.md`. Give the form's error and success words in `COPY.md`.

A form must really send. If there is no back end, use a direct link.

**Phone:** one column, details first.

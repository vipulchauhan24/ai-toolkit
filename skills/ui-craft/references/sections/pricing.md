# Pricing

**Job:** say what it costs without a fight.

| Variant | Use when |
|---|---|
| `plans` | Two to four plans, using `../components/pricing-table.md` |
| `single` | One price, large, with what's included |
| `list` | Services or fees in rows |
| `quote` | No public price: how it's scoped and how fast a quote comes |

Tone and words: flat and factual. Terms sit by the button.

**Phone:** stacked.

# Case study page

**Job:** show how you think, on a page of its own.

**Layout:** title and a short summary block at the top (role, team, time, result). Body in `--prose`. Images can break out to `--container`.

**Sections:** problem, role, constraints, approach and rejected options, result, what you learned. Headings state the point.

Link to the next project at the end.

**Phone:** the summary block stacks. Images run the full content width.

**Avoid:** a wall of screens with no words. A straight line from problem to solution with no doubt or trade-off.

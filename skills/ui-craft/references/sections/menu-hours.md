# Menu and hours

**Job:** answer "what do you have, what does it cost, and are you open".

**Menu:** real text with prices. Sections of dishes as rows with dotted or plain alignment: name left, price right in tabular figures, a short description below in `--ink-2`. Two columns from 1024px when long.

**Hours:** a simple list, day left, time right. Closed days stated. One source in the code, used everywhere.

**Phone:** one column. Hours and address appear within the first screen or two.

**Avoid:** PDF menus. Hours only inside an image.

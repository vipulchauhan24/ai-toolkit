# Experience

**Job:** show where you've worked and what changed because of you.

| Variant | Use when |
|---|---|
| `timeline` | A clean history, using `../components/timeline.md` |
| `rows` | Company and dates left, details right. Rows split by 1px lines |
| `accordion` | Long careers: the latest role is open, the rest closed |
| `by-company` | Several projects per employer, grouped under the company |

Each role: title, company, dates, two to four bullets that state a change. Tools as small tags or a plain line. Bullets at `--text-sm` to `--text-base`, up to `--prose`.

**Phone:** dates above each entry.

**Avoid:** large boxes of dense small text. Equal weight for a role from ten years ago and the current one.

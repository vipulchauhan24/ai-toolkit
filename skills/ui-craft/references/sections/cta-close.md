# Closing call to action

**Job:** give one last clear step, tied to the page's main promise.

| Variant | Use when |
|---|---|
| `band` | A contrasting band with a short heading and a button |
| `quiet` | One line and a link, for portfolios and content |
| `form` | The action is a short form: email or booking |

Same button wording as the hero. Restate the promise with one fact.

**Leave out** on short pages where the contact section already does this.

**Avoid:** "Ready to get started?" with nothing specific.

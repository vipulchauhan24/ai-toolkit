# Waitlist

**Job:** earn an email for something that isn't out yet.

**Layout:** one screen is usually enough. `centered` or `split` hero, one email field and a button on one line from 640px, with what they get right beside it.

Show something real: a screen, a demo, or a clear explanation of the problem.

The success state replaces the form and says what happens next.

**Avoid:** countdowns with no real date. Fake sign-up counts.

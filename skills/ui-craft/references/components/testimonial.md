# Testimonial

**Use for** a real quote, word for word, from a named person. No real quote means no testimonial.

- Quote `--text-lead` to `--text-h3`, in `--ink`. Mark up with `blockquote`.
- Name `--text-sm`, weight 500. Role and company in `--ink-2`.
- A portrait only if it's real: 40 to 48px circle.
- Keep the speaker's own wording, including any roughness.

One strong quote can stand alone, large. Several work as a simple list or a two-column grid. Their lengths will differ, so let them.

**Avoid:** five-star graphics with no source. Three matching cards. Sliders that rotate.

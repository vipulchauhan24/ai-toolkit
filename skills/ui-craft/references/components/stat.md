# Stat

**Use for** a real number that matters, with enough context to judge it.

- Value `--text-h2` on websites, 24 to 32px in apps. Tabular figures.
- Label `--text-sm` in `--ink-2`, directly beside or under the value, `--space-1` to `--space-2` away.
- A context line where needed: what it compares with, or where it came from.

Every number is real and in the fact sheet. A number means more next to its before, unit or comparison.

Group two to four related stats, or put each one in the sentence or card it supports.

**Phone:** two columns, or one list with label left and value right.

**Avoid:** a strip of round numbers on every page. Icons in coloured circles.

# Footer component

The bar at the bottom of every page. For its variants see `../sections/footer.md`.

- Uses `--container` and `--gutter`.
- Padding `--space-12` to `--space-16` top and bottom.
- Text `--text-sm`, in `--ink-2`.
- Links in the footer have a tap area of at least `--touch` on touch screens.
- Holds facts only: name, year, legal links, contact, and social links that exist.

**Avoid:** a one-line sign-off or joke. Columns of links to pages that don't exist.

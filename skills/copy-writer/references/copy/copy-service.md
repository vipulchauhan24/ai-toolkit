# Copy for agencies, consultants and other hired services

The product is people and judgement, and the visitor can't try it first. So the job is to reduce risk: show the work, show who does it, and show exactly what happens if they get in touch.

Covers agencies, studios, consultants, freelancers, clinics, law firms, trades.

## Who reads it

Someone with a problem and a budget, usually comparing two or three providers, and often worried about getting it wrong.

Their questions, in order:

1. What exactly do you do, and for whom?
2. Have you done this for someone like me?
3. Did it work?
4. Who would I actually be working with?
5. How does it work, and what happens after I pay?
6. What will it cost, roughly?
7. How do I start?

The fifth is the most often unanswered, and one of the most useful to answer.

## Fact sheet: ask for these

- The one or two things you are best at. "Everything for everyone" is not a position.
- The clients you are right for, and those you turn away.
- 2 or 3 projects with client names (if allowed), the problem, the approach and the result.
- The people who do the work.
- The steps from first call to finished work, with typical times.
- Price, a starting figure, or how it is scoped.
- Credentials, licences and insurance that are real.

## Sections

| Section | Job | How to write it |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | Who you help, with what | h1 names the service and the client: "Brand identity for restaurants" |
| Services | What you can be hired for | A plain list. Each item says what the client receives |
| Work or case studies | Proof | 2 or 3, relevant. Problem, approach, result |
| Process | What happens after they say yes | Numbered steps: what happens, who does it, how long |
| Team | Who they will meet | Real names, photos, what each does |
| Pricing | Saves everyone time | See below |
| Testimonials | Other people's words | Real, word for word, with name and role |
| FAQ | Remaining worries | Scope, timing, revisions, what you need from them |
| Contact | The action | Say what happens next and how soon |

## Positioning comes before wording

Narrow copy reads as expertise. "Marketing agency" could be anyone. "We rebuild the sites of family-run hotels" is someone. If the client won't narrow, at least name who their best work was for.

Replace claims with rules that can be checked:

| Claim | Rule |
|---|---|
| "We care about quality" | "Every drawing is checked by a second engineer" |
| "Client-focused" | "You speak to the person doing the work, every week" |
| "Fast turnaround" | "First draft in five working days" |

Only write rules the client confirms.

## Pricing

Many agencies don't publish prices, and opinion is split on whether they should. If there is a figure the client is willing to show, show it: a "from" price or a typical range filters out bad-fit enquiries. If not, say how pricing works and how quickly they will get a quote.

## Case studies

Lead with the result if it is real, then how you got there.

- The client and their problem, in a sentence or two.
- What you did. Specific decisions, not a list of services.
- What changed, with numbers if the client allows it.
- A quote from the client if there is one.

## Contact

Say exactly what happens: "Tell us about the project. Sam reads every message and replies within two working days with a time to talk." (Use only a time the client confirms.)

Regulated trades: law, health and finance have rules on claims, testimonials and results. Ask the client what they may say, and do not promise outcomes.

## Tone

Assured and plain. Professional services can be warmer in the about text and must be flat on fees and terms. A clinic or a law firm is calm first, personal second. A creative studio can show more character, as long as a stranger still understands what they do.

## Checklist

- [ ] The headline names the service and the client type.
- [ ] A reader can see what happens after they get in touch.
- [ ] The people are named.
- [ ] Each case study has a result, or says why not.
- [ ] Every claim is either a fact or a rule the client confirmed.
